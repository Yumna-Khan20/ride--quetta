Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about RideQuetta, our booking process, and demo mode.

Enter your pickup and destination in the booking form to see an upfront fare estimate. When you submit your request, a unique booking reference code is generated. You will then await confirmation through our system or configured dispatchers before your ride starts.
The estimate is calculated using road route distance between your chosen Quetta coordinates combined with illustrative base rates (Rs. 70 base + Rs. 35/km). Actual final fares may vary depending on actual traffic conditions, diversions, or route confirmation.
Until RideQuetta's production database (Supabase) is connected by the site owner, the site operates in local demo mode. In this mode, booking and driver registration requests are saved safely in your browser's local storage and are not dispatched to real drivers.
Local rickshaw drivers can complete the "Drive With Us" registration form on this website. Our team reviews applicant details, vehicle plates, and preferred zones before onboarding operators into the booking network.
We collect only the essential details required to coordinate your ride: your name, phone number, and pickup/destination points. We do not sell or share passenger data with third-party advertisers.
